# Homepage media slot and buyer-path plan

## Business understanding

Hefei Baite Optoelectronics Technology Co., Ltd. presents WCBEST mineral optical sorting machinery. The verified catalogue contains four independent machines covering powder, AI mineral, dry rapid-belt and wet/dry integrated rapid-belt sorting. Procurement roles are inferred as mineral-processing plant buyers, process/engineering reviewers and distributors; those roles are design hypotheses, not customer-confirmed market claims. Buyer concerns are model fit, readable operating parameters, supplier identity, manufacturing evidence, management-system certificates, installed-equipment evidence and a usable technical inquiry route.

## Page content matrix

- /: complete buyer path and representative evidence.
- /products: all four real records with classification and comparison cues.
- /products/[slug]: matched images, description, full supplied parameter table, application and product-aware RFQ.
- /applications: use-case selection without claiming a successful project.
- /technology: neutral process explanation anchored to supplied product facts.
- /manufacturing: all qualified factory imagery and verified facility metrics.
- /cases: twelve anonymous installed-equipment records from the customer’s old site; no invented customer names or results.
- /certifications: each management-system certificate separated; all six pages inspectable.
- /about: company identity, business scope, verified facility context and contact.
- /news and /news/[slug]: backend-driven; honest empty state until real articles exist.
- /contact: full company context and balanced RFQ form.

## Optional section decisions

- Updates: retain News capability but no demo articles.
- Trust strip: merge verified facility metrics and certificates; no customer-logo strip.
- Custom value/options: merge into product detail and inquiry; do not invent OEM dimensions.
- Cooperation process: use supported FAQ facts only.
- Applications: use as a real independent route.
- Social channels: none supplied; do not show empty icons.
